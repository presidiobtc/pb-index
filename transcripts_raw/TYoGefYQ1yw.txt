[00:55:37] Stable Channels pitch. Tony presents Stable Channels as a way to deliver stable dollar balances on bitcoin while preserving self-custody, peer-to-peer architecture, and decentralization.

[00:56:01] Stablecoin product-market fit and bitcoin ethos. Stable Channels starts from the observation that stablecoins have undeniable product-market fit as dollar bank accounts, trading base pairs, and medium of exchange, but are fiat IOUs with intermediaries and balance-sheet risk.

[00:57:12] Stable Channels mechanism. The protocol pairs two bitcoin holders in a Lightning channel: one wants stable dollar value, and the other provides stability by taking the opposite, effectively leveraged-long exposure. Both run Lightning nodes and stable channels software.

[00:58:17] Lightning rebalancing. Stable Channels checks exchange price feeds every thirty seconds and streams sats between the receiver and provider as bitcoin's price moves, keeping one side at the target dollar value and giving the other leveraged bitcoin exposure.

[00:59:27] Streaming finance. Tony argues Lightning compresses counterparty risk down to seconds, enabling financial relationships that update continuously in tiny increments. Stable Channels can support dollar stability, allocation mixes, interest payments, and payments in and out over Lightning.

[01:00:44] Stable Channels portfolio allocation. Users can choose mixes of bitcoin and dollar exposure, such as 25% bitcoin and 75% dollar exposure, and rebalance between those positions through the channel.

[01:01:24] Stable Channels privacy and tax framing. Tony says the architecture may help financial privacy by using bitcoin's peer-to-peer and self-custody model, and may be tax efficient in some jurisdictions because users enter a synthetic dollar position rather than selling bitcoin outright.

[01:02:01] Bitcoin derivative settlement. Stable Channels imagines future exposures beyond bitcoin and dollars, such as gold or the S&P 500, using bitcoin as the settlement mechanism and funding rates similar to perpetual futures.
