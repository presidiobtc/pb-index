About

Presidio Bitcoin is a coworking and event space in San Francisco for builders and startups working toward a more private and decentralized future across bitcoin and AI. We serve as a physical hub for this community, and as a bridge to Silicon Valley and the broader world of serious builders and thinkers.

In addition to providing a shared workspace for those building in and around bitcoin and AI, Presidio Bitcoin is a home for insightful, high-signal events and media. Our programming ranges from monthly technical meetups to major, multidisciplinary, industry-leading summits. Our iconic podcast studio powers our flagship shows, which capture, present, and preserve the ideas that emerge from this ecosystem.

This site is a repository of the talks, podcasts, summits, and events we produce so ideas are easy to reference, share and ultimately, we hope, build on.
