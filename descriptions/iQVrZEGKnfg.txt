At Presidio Bitcoin's Quantum Bitcoin Summit, this keynote by Jameson Lopp (Casa) explores the intersection of quantum computing and Bitcoin's ossification. He outlines the case for long-term preparedness, emphasizing that Bitcoin's slow-moving consensus process should keep pace with the fast-appearing CRQC threat.

Lopp walks through quantum scenarios and the dilemmas in trade-offs between Bitcoin’s core values along with questions around quantum vulnerable coins and the philosophical and game-theoretic knots facing key ecosystem stakeholders.

Issues explored:
– Migration incentives and recovery schemes
– The block size debate’s return
– Wealth redistribution from "quantum theft"
– Soft forks vs. miner-driven enforcement
– Opt-in security vs. ecosystem-wide volatility
