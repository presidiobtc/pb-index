The future of money is inseparable from the future of payments. Will the next generation of global money apps be built on Bitcoin’s decentralized rails or on stablecoins, dollar-backed tokens with growing regulatory clarity? 

In this session, Nick Slaney makes the case for Bitcoin as the most accessible, censorship-resistant system for payments, while Daniel Nordh argues for stablecoins as interoperable rails that align with user habits and compliance regimes. The discussion explores the challenges of building a truly global payments app, the role of self-custody, the tradeoffs between stable value and censorship resistance, the UX of on-ramps and off-ramps, and the impact of emerging regulations.
