Subtitle: Why Bitcoin lending keeps drifting back to centralization and how new tailwinds can change that.

In our newest episode of Built in the Presidio, we discuss Surge with CEO Yash Belavadi and Chief Product Officer Michael Borglin. The two Presidio Bitcoin members unpack how Surge is solving one of Bitcoin's most stubborn failure modes: custodial lending.

Bitcoin-backed loans are a millenia-old financial idea with a modern upgrade - the use of programmable, verifiable collateral - Bitcoin. Nevertheless, most Bitcoin lending products still require users to hand over their coins to opaque custodians. This has led to a predictable cycle of fast growth during bull markets, followed by large implosions.

In this conversation, Yash and Michael cover how they're building a non-custodial lending product that leverages new technical developments along with strategies for bootstrapping liquidity that don't require centralized shortcuts.

Other topics covered include:
- Why Bitcoin lending keeps drifting back to centralization and custodians - and why those models keep blowing up
- Lessons from BlockFi and FTX, and what they changed
- The legal and regulatory shifts in how non-custodial protocols can operate
- ETF-based lending and what it means for retail
- Surge's approach to on-chain risk parameters and distributed custody
- Surge user product journey
- Challenges around solving the liquidity cold start in a two-sided marketplace

Surge embodies Presidio Bitcoin's core ethos: Bitcoin isn't just a financial asset, but a technology that enables better, safer, and more open systems and infrastructure.
