In the closing panel at Presidio Bitcoin's Quantum Bitcoin Summit, Steve Lee (Spiral, Lead; Presidio Bitcoin, Co-Founder), Clara Shikhelman (Chaincode Labs, Head of Research) and Tadge Dryja (Lightning Network Co-Inventor) explore next steps for each Bitcoin stakeholder group: users, developers, businesses, miners, and investors, in light of quantum risks. 

Topics include: address reuse, burn vs freeze, developer reluctance, wallet UX, roadmap uncertainty, and the value of continued discussion.

The session includes both pragmatic, actionable advice and philosophical debate on how the Bitcoin community can prepare for an uncertain quantum future.
