This panel examines whether model weights and AI outputs qualify as protected speech, how AI regulation intersects with the First Amendment, and whether people have a right to run intelligence on hardware they own. The conversation explores the limits of “code is speech,” the privacy advantages of local AI, and why technical experts should help shape policy before new regulatory frameworks are established.

Panelists: Greg Lukianoff (President & CEO, FIRE) · Conner Brown (Executive Director, Bitcoin Policy Institute) · Kyle Olney (Senior Advisor, Bitcoin Policy Institute)
