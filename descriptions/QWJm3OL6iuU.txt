As AI becomes embedded across the enterprise, a company’s most valuable assets increasingly include what it uniquely knows: proprietary data, trade secrets, institutional knowledge, intellectual property, and the collective intelligence built over decades. This talk explores how open-source AI and sovereign data infrastructure can allow organizations to capture the transformative value of AI without surrendering control of the knowledge that creates their competitive advantage.
