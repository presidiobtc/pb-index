Era Qian sat down with Prime Intellect co-founder Vincent Weisser to discuss his journey building the company and its mission to democratize the infrastructure behind advanced AI. They explore distributed training, open-source post-training, and why giving developers and businesses ownership of their models could offer an alternative to centralized AI labs.
