In this inaugural episode of Built in the Presidio, Vora founders Jesse Posner and Erik Cason discuss the next frontier of self-sovereignty: verifiable hardware and private, self-hosted, user-owned AI. What began as a critique of cheap, opaque hardware wallets evolved into a deeper mission: building a fully auditable, open-source computing stack capable of safeguarding both Bitcoin and personal data.

They explore supply-chain integrity, chip-level verification, and the philosophical roots of crypto-sovereignty and then extend those ideas into a future where individuals run their own encrypted, fiduciary AIs. The conversation touches on the coming battle between privacy and surveillance, the legal foundations for cryptographic freedom, and why the Bitcoin ethos may define the architecture of the digital age.
