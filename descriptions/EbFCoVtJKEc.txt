Christoph Ono, co-founder of the Bitcoin Design Foundation and reflects on five years of the Bitcoin Design Community and the challenges that still lie ahead.

In this talk, delivered at Presidio Bitcoin's Bitcoin Design Week, Christoph explores why designing Bitcoin is uniquely hard:

Bridging the gap between today’s 100M users and billions worldwide
The psychological, cultural, and regulatory complexities of money
Tensions between sovereignty and safety nets, simplicity and resilience
Why design is mission-critical in an adversarial, global environment

A fantastic, candid, big-picture look at what it will take for Bitcoin to become usable and empowering for people everywhere.
