Every security guarantee in an AI system eventually rests on silicon almost no one can inspect. Three teams working that problem share their approaches. Vora is building a personal AI appliance with its own secure element. Caution is building verifiable compute out of Distrust’s open-source supply-chain work. Sudo’s Project SOVereign is an attempt at a maximally open source, fully verifiable, security-first chip.
