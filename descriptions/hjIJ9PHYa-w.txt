In this episode of PBJ, the crew explores the path to a Type 1 Civilization powered by Bitcoin, AI, and solar — and previews the upcoming Type 1 Summit at Presidio Bitcoin. They break down the Billionaire Flippening thesis, where bitcoin billionaires surpass fiat billionaires and redirect wealth into things like open-source alternatives and philanthropy. Finally, they unpack Apple’s new iPhone security upgrade, Memory Integrity Enforcement (MIE), and how it reflects bitcoin’s ethos of privacy and resilience.
