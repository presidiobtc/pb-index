At Presidio Bitcoin’s Type I Summit, Mike “Shrep” Schroepfer (Gigascale), and Garth Sheldon-Coulson (Panthalassa), discuss building data centers and power systems out at sea: tapping wave energy for cheap, abundant electricity, natural cooling, and rapid scaling without grid constraints. They dig into why the ocean could support 10-15 terawatts of clean power, how AI inference and Bitcoin mining can operate off-grid, and what energy abundance means for the future.
