At Presidio Bitcoin's Quantum Bitcoin Summit Terry Rudolph, a pioneering photonic physicist and co-founder of the world’s largest quantum computing startup, PsiQuantum, joined Sho Sugiura (Blocq) and Matthew Pines (Bitcoin Policy Institute) to assess timelines, architectures, and the future of ECDSA security.

Featuring:
Terry Rudolph (Co-Founder and Chief Architect, PsiQuantum)
Sho Sugiura (Co-Founder and CEO, Blocq)
Matthew Pines (Moderator, Bitcoin Policy Institute)

Topics covered:
Realistic timelines for cryptographically relevant quantum computers (CRQCs)
Which hardware paths are still in the race?
Why ECDSA could fall by 2030.
Government secrecy vs. public roadmaps: should Bitcoiners trust the commercial timelines?
What milestones to watch: logical qubits, cryo bottlenecks, and entangled photonic states.
Philosophical detours
