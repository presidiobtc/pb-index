Join us inside Bitcoin Design Week 2025 at Presidio Bitcoin in San Francisco. Over five days, designers, builders, and Bitcoiners explored what it means to design future money, from user needs around the world, to stablecoins and payments, vibe coding, grand design challenges, and imagining how Bitcoin might live inside Silicon Valley’s biggest products.

In this recap, Mogashni Naidoo, Christoph Ono, and Mat Balez share the key highlights, lessons, and insights from the week:
Who’s using Bitcoin today and why
Bitcoin vs. stablecoins as the future of payments
The rise of vibe coding and rapid prototyping
Tackling Bitcoin’s biggest design challenges
Imagining Bitcoin inside Gmail, Uber, LinkedIn, and more
