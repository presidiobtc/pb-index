yellowpages: A Live Post-Quantum Recovery Protocol for Bitcoin

At Presidio Bitcoin's Quantum Bitcoin Summit, Project 11 revealed yellowpages - the first live infrastructure for proving Bitcoin ownership in a post-quantum world.

In this demo, Alex Pruden outlines a novel framework for users to assert self-sovereignty over ECDSA UTXOs, even in the wake of a cryptographic break. Built on dual post-quantum signature schemes (MLDSA and SLHDSA), trusted execution environments (TEE), and hybrid encryption using ML-KEM, yellowpages enables social recovery and off-chain claims without revealing public keys.
