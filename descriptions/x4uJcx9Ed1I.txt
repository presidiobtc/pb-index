Presidio Bitcoin’s Type I Summit brought together Banks Hunter (Charge Robotics), Alex Fuster (Astro Energy), Ali Chehrehsaz (TerraVerde Energy) for a practical deep dive on how to scale solar from gigawatts to terawatts. They unpack land and interconnection constraints, labor bottlenecks/solar-construction robots, microgrids vs legacy utilities, materials and recycling, and what ultra-cheap solar could mean for water, manufacturing, and the future grid.
