This panel explores how AI-powered security scanning is reshaping the race between attackers and defenders in open-source software. The conversation covers finding and triaging vulnerabilities, responsible disclosure, protecting maintainers from malicious reports, and why access to open-weight models is becoming essential to defending critical code.

Panelists: Steve Lee (Lead, Spiral) · Elias Rohrer (Developer, Spiral) · Jordan Mecom (Staff Security Engineer, Block) · Rob Hamilton (Co-Founder & CEO, AnchorWatch)
