How can Bitcoin move beyond “digital gold” and truly work as money at the register? In this session, Haley Berkoe from Spiral explores the design challenge of inspiring merchants to accept Bitcoin payments. The talk covers why adoption has lagged, the role of grassroots onboarding, and how better branding, creativity, and tools like Square can make Bitcoin payments easy, fun, and rewarding for businesses.
