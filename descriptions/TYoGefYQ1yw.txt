Key ideas:
- Bitcoin startup investing has lagged because the number of investable companies, venture-scale categories, and mainstream customer use cases has been limited so far.
- Exchanges, mining hardware, custody-adjacent products, lending, and holding bitcoin have been the clearest early business models.
- The next wave may come from net-new use cases that only bitcoin can enable: global 24/7 payments, self-custodied AI, non-custodial credit, Lightning-native finance, merchant adoption, and Nostr identity.
- Developer tools have improved enough that bitcoin applications can now be prototyped quickly with LDK, Lightspark, Lexe-style services, and AI coding tools.
- Vora presents self-custodied AI: personal AI hardware secured with user-owned keys, private data, verifiable software, and bitcoin-style recovery.
- Castle automates bitcoin treasury allocation for businesses through revenue automation, sweep accounts, treasury thresholds, and integrations with tools such as Stripe, Square, PayPal, Shopify, MindBody, Clover, QuickBooks, and eBay.
- Stable Channels proposes self-custodied dollar-denominated balances on Lightning by pairing users who want dollar stability with providers who want leveraged bitcoin exposure.
- Guarana Bank presents a shared-custody bitcoin-backed credit app for Latin America, shaped by Argentina's history of currency devaluation and the need to borrow without selling bitcoin or sending collateral to a black-box custodian.
