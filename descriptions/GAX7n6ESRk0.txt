This panel explores how open-weight models could compete with closed AI labs and how autonomous agents may earn, transact, and hold resources. The conversation covers control over data, the economics of compute, verification, bitcoin, and why a diverse ecosystem of open models may be safer than relying on a few centralized systems.

Panelists: Max Webster (Founder, Hivemind Ventures) · John Komkov (Managing Partner, Fathom Capital) · Christian Catalini (Founder, MIT Cryptoeconomics Lab)
