Drawing on two foundational papers:  

51% Attack via Difficulty Increase with a Small Quantum Miner (2024): https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.08023
On the insecurity of quantum Bitcoin mining (2018): https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.08118 

This talk by Or Sattath, an associate professor at the Ben-Gurion Computer Science Department, offers one of the most comprehensive analyses to date of Bitcoin’s vulnerability to quantum-accelerated mining.

Presented at Presidio Bitcoin's Quantum Bitcoin Summit, this session explores why quantum mining is not just the next step after ASICs and how Grover’s algorithm fundamentally alters mining economics, fork dynamics, and security assumptions.
