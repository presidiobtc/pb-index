In this presentation from Presidio Bitcoin's Quantum Bitcoin Summit, Tadge Dryja, co-inventor of the Lightning Network, introduces Lifeboat, a lightweight commit/reveal mechanism designed to protect Bitcoin users from short-range quantum attacks, where a quantum adversary learns a public key after it appears in a transaction. Drawing from earlier work like Fawkescoin, Lifeboat enables secure spending without requiring a full post-quantum signature scheme, using a three-hash commitment model (AID, SDP, CTXID) and minimal changes to Bitcoin’s consensus rules.

Dryja walks through the threat model, commitment validation process, node storage implications, and attack scenarios. He also discusses deployment challenges, long-term relevance, and how Lifeboat complements future PQ migration strategies.

Key themes: quantum resistance, commit/reveal, short-range attacks, soft-fork design, post-quantum Bitcoin security.
