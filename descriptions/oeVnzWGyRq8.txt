As quantum computing advances, Bitcoin's current digital signatures (ECDSA/Schnorr) face real vulnerabilities. In this technical session, Olaoluwa "Lalu" Osuntokun (Lightning Labs) offers a deep dive into hash-based signature schemes, especially SPHINCS+, as a resilient post-quantum alternative.

Covering:
Why hash-based signatures (vs lattice/code-based)?
How SPHINCS+ works: WOTS+, FORS, XMSS, and hypertrees
Stateless security and the end of public key derivation
Parameter tuning for Bitcoin use cases
Implementation trade-offs and wallet implications
Signature size optimizations and potential BIPs
Multisig and MPC limitations
Thoughts on STARK-based aggregation and future integrations
