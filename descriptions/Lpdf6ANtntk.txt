At Presidio Bitcoin’s Grand Design Challenges session, Joshua Philippe (Head of Design & Brand at Fold; formerly BitMEX and Strike) unpacks the branding challenges facing Bitcoin.

In this rapid-fire talk, he shares:
His personal journey 
Why Bitcoin’s current visual identity is fragmented and immature
The cultural and political pitfalls of Bitcoin’s image
Lessons from mainstream brands like Cash App, Gemini, and Apple
A live design challenge: rethinking the credit card chip as a Bitcoin brand artifact

Josh argues that Bitcoin’s brand must evolve beyond tech circles, speaking to culture at large while balancing inclusivity, usability, and identity.
