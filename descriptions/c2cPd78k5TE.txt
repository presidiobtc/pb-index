Political philosopher Michael Millerman joins Vora co-founder Jesse Posner to explore how thinkers from Plato and Aristotle to Strauss and Heidegger can help us understand AI. They examine open versus closed systems, the concentration of power, and the deeper questions AI raises about knowledge, freedom, and human nature.

Recorded at the Open Source AI Summit, September 10–11, 2026, in San Francisco’s Presidio. The summit brought together 150 builders and advocates to explore open, private, and local AI across technology, science, policy, economics, and philosophy. opensourceaisummit.org
