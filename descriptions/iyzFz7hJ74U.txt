At Presidio Bitcoin’s Type I Summit former Meta CTO Mike Schroepfer (Schrep) breaks down why building data centers is harder than people think, and why offshore, ship-like systems could cut complexity with power next to compute, ocean-scale cooling, and massive untapped energy. Panthalassa’s Garth Sheldon-Coulson also walks through the first-principles case, costs, and why this is approach best suited for AI inference.
