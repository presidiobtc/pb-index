Bitcoin miners were the first to secure large-scale land, power, and data infrastructure in places no one else would go. Today, that infrastructure is becoming the foundation of America’s AI data center boom.

This discussion at Presidio Bitcoin's Type I Summit with Andrew Myers (Satoshi Energy), Brent Whitehead (Giga Energy), and Brad Cuddy (Cholla Inc.) explores how Bitcoin mining evolved from the grid’s “buyer of last resort” to a foundational force in America’s fastest-growing data center industry, and what that shift means for the grid, energy markets, and the future of compute.
