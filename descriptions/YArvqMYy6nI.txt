Kyle Fletcher, former Head of Brand Creative at Cash App and now a principal design director at Block, breaks down a decade of Bitcoin-facing brand work and what it can teach the ecosystem. Kyle maps the evolution of Bitcoin aesthetics and offers a five-part “aesthetic horizon”, then spotlights artists and inspirations that can push Bitcoin creative forward.

If you’re a designer, marketer, or Bitcoiner thinking about culture and adoption, this talk is a fast tour of what’s worked, what hasn’t, and where to experiment next so we can reach the next 96% through everyday experiences, not just apps.
