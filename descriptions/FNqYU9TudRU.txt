In this presentation from Presidio Bitcoin's Quantum Bitcoin Summit, Clara Shikhelman, Head of Research at Chaincode Labs, breaks down the practical implications of quantum computing for Bitcoin’s long-term security model, anchored by her wide circulated co-authored report: https://chaincode.com/bitcoin-post-quantum.pdf. 

This presentation offers one of the clearest and most comprehensive technical walkthroughs of the Bitcoin-quantum intersection, emphasizing both the engineering challenge and the philosophical questions that quantum threats introduce to Bitcoin, covering signatures, mining, migration, and mitigation. 

Check out more of Dr. Shikhelman's Bitcoin-quantum work: https://pq-bitcoin.org/
